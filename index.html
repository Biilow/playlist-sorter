Sort a playlist by the colour of its album covers.

White first, through the rainbow, down to black. Or sort by artist, BPM, key, mood or a full listening arc.

Spotify asks each person to create their own free developer app. It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once. Your Spotify account needs Premium.

  1. Open Spotify’s form

    Open the Create app page in a new tab and log in with your Spotify account.

  2. Fill it in

    Copy each of these into the matching box.

    1 App name
    Playlist Sorter
    2 App description
    Sorts my playlists
    3 Redirect URI, then press Add

    Tick 4 Web API and 5 the terms, then press 6 Save.

  3. Copy your Client ID

    Spotify now shows your new app. Press the copy icon next to the Client ID, in the box at the top. You never need the client secret, so keep that private.

  4. Connect

Try it on a sample playlist first

If you reorder a playlist and Spotify still shows the old order, close and reopen the Spotify app.

This site has no server, accounts or tracking. Your Spotify login stays in this browser tab and ends when you close it. Only song IDs are sent to ReccoBeats, for BPM, key and mood.
